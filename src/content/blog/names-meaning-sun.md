Almost every culture has a name for the sun, and almost every culture has turned it into a name for a child. That makes sun names unusually varied: you can choose something as familiar as Sunny or Aurora, or as rare as Heulwen or Inti.

Below are 45 names that mean sun, sunshine, or dawn, grouped by meaning. Each includes its language of origin and a pronunciation guide, since many of these will be new to English speakers.

> 📌 **Key Takeaway:** If you want a sun name that is easy to use in English, look at Sol, Elio, Soleil, Ravi, and Aurora. If you want something rare, Welsh (Heulwen), Armenian (Arev), and Quechua (Inti) each have a simple word for sun that works as a name. To complete the sky, see our lists of [names meaning moon](/blog/names-meaning-moon/) and [names meaning star](/blog/names-meaning-star/).

## How to Choose a Sun Name

**Pair it with the season.** A sun name suits a summer baby, but it can be even more meaningful for a baby born in the darkest part of winter, close to the solstice, when the days begin to lengthen again.

**Check the spelling against the sound.** Soleil is said so-LAY and Heulwen is HAYL-wen. If a name is not pronounced the way it looks in English, be ready to spell it and say it often.

**Consider a sun and moon pair for siblings or twins.** Sol and Luna, Elio and Selene, and Ravi and Chandra are matching pairs without being matching sounds. Our [twin baby name pairs](/blog/twin-baby-name-pairs/) guide has more.

**Be careful with names from living religious traditions.** Surya, Aditya, and Savitri are names of deities who are actively worshipped. They are common given names in Hindu families, and using them with respect means knowing what they refer to.

## FAQ

### What girl name means sun?

Soleil is French for sun, Helia is the feminine form of the Greek word for sun, and Sunniva means sun gift. Heulwen is Welsh for sunshine, and Arevik is Armenian for little sun.

### What boy name means sun?

Elio and Helios come from the Greek word for sun. Ravi, Surya, and Mihir mean sun in Sanskrit, Shams means sun in Arabic, and Samson comes from the Hebrew word for sun.

### What name means sunshine?

Heulwen is the Welsh word for sunshine, and Aftab means sun or sunshine in Persian and Urdu. In English, Sunny and Sunshine are both used as given names.

### What names mean dawn?

Aurora is Latin for dawn, Zora is Slavic, Alba is Italian and Spanish, and Eos is Greek. Roxana comes from a Persian word meaning dawn or bright.
