Some babies arrive after a long wait: after loss, after years of trying, after a pregnancy that kept everyone holding their breath. For many parents, the name becomes a way of saying what the child means to them.

This list gathers 50 names whose meaning is *miracle*, *gift*, or *blessing*, along with names traditionally given to a child who was asked for. Each entry has its origin and literal meaning. Where a meaning is debated by scholars, we say so, because you deserve to know exactly what you are choosing.

> 📌 **Key Takeaway:** "Gift of God" is one of the most common name meanings in the world, and it hides inside many familiar names: Matthew, Theodore, Dorothy, Nathaniel, and Jonathan all carry it. If you want the meaning without announcing it, those are the names to look at. For related ideas, see our lists of [names meaning hope](/blog/names-meaning-hope/) and [names meaning grace](/blog/names-meaning-grace/).

## Names for a Long-Awaited or Rainbow Baby

A rainbow baby is a baby born after a miscarriage, stillbirth, or infant loss. These names speak to waiting, asking, and the light after a storm.

| Name | Gender | Origin | Meaning | Say it |
|------|--------|--------|---------|--------|
| Iris | Girl | Greek | "Rainbow"; Iris was the goddess of the rainbow | EYE-ris |
| Samuel | Boy | Hebrew | Usually explained as "God has heard"; in the Bible, Hannah names her son Samuel because she asked God for him | SAM-yoo-ul |
| Saul | Boy | Hebrew | "Asked for, prayed for" | SAWL |
| Hannah | Girl | Hebrew | "Grace, favor"; the mother who prayed for a child | HAN-uh |
| Anastasia | Girl | Greek | "Resurrection" | an-uh-STAY-zhuh |
| Renata | Girl | Latin | "Reborn" | reh-NAH-tah |

Some parents choose a name that remembers the child who came before, while others prefer a name that belongs only to this baby. There is no right answer, and you do not owe anyone an explanation for your choice.

## How to Choose a Name with This Meaning

**Decide how visible you want the meaning to be.** Miracle and Blessing say it out loud. Theodore and Beatrice carry the same idea quietly, and your child can share the story when they choose to.

**Think about how your child will feel about it at fifteen.** A name that carries a heavy story can feel like a lot to live up to. A classic name with a meaningful root lets the story be theirs to tell.

**Use the middle name for the meaning.** Clara Milagros, Henry Asher, Rose Dorothea. The first name can be chosen for sound and the middle name for significance.

**Check the pronunciation in your community.** Names like Bracha and Baruch contain a sound that does not exist in English, and they are most at home in families who use Hebrew.

## FAQ

### What name means miracle?

Mira and Mirabel come from the Latin root meaning wonderful, the same root as the word miracle. Milagros is Spanish for miracles, Karishma means miracle in Hindi and Urdu, and Alazne means miracle in Basque.

### What name means gift from God?

Matthew, Theodore, Dorothy, Dorothea, Theodora, Nathaniel, and Jonathan all mean gift of God or God has given. Theodore and Dorothea are built from the same two Greek words in opposite order.

### What is a good name for a rainbow baby?

Iris means rainbow in Greek and is the most direct choice. Other names parents choose include Asher (happy, blessed), Samuel (God has heard), Mira (wonderful), and Renata (reborn).

### What boy name means blessed?

Benedict and its English form Bennett mean blessed, as do Asher and Baruch from Hebrew. Barack comes from a word meaning blessing.
