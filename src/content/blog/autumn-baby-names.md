Autumn names have a particular warmth. They come from turning leaves, harvest fields, and the reds and golds that only show up for a few weeks a year. If your baby is due between September and November, a fall-inspired name is a quiet way to mark the season they arrived in.

This list covers 48 names in four groups: trees and plants, warm colors, harvest and mythology, and names tied to the autumn months themselves. Every name includes its origin and literal meaning, because a name that only *sounds* autumnal is different from one that actually means something connected to the season.

> 📌 **Key Takeaway:** The strongest autumn names fall into two families: nature names (Hazel, Rowan, Maple, Aspen) and red-gold color names (Sienna, Russell, Rory, Aurelia). Many of the "red" names, like Rory, Flynn, and Rufus, originally described red hair, which is why they feel so at home in fall. For names from every season, see our guide to [baby names by season](/blog/baby-names-by-season/).

## How to Choose an Autumn Name That Ages Well

**Check the literal meaning, not just the mood.** Some names feel autumnal only because of a movie or a color palette. Names in this list are here because their meaning connects to the season.

**Say it with your surname.** Short nature names like Ash, Reed, and Wren pair best with longer surnames. Longer names like Persephone and Aurelia balance a short surname.

**Consider the middle spot.** If Maple or October feels too bold as a first name, it can be a lovely middle name: Clara Maple, Henry October.

**Think about nicknames.** Aurelia becomes Aura or Lia, Demetria becomes Demi, Russell becomes Russ. Make sure you like the short form too, because that is what the playground will use.

## FAQ

### What is a good name for a baby born in autumn?

Hazel, Rowan, Sienna, Aurelia, and Jasper are popular choices with a real connection to the season. Hazel and Rowan are tree names, Sienna and Aurelia are warm color names, and Jasper is a red-brown gemstone.

### What names mean autumn or fall?

Autumn is the only common English name that literally means the season. Most fall-inspired names instead come from things associated with it: trees (Maple, Aspen), harvest goddesses (Demetria, Ceres, Pomona), or red and gold colors (Russell, Rory, Aurelia).

### What are unisex autumn baby names?

Rowan, Aspen, Sage, Rory, Auburn, Oakley, and Linden are all used for both boys and girls.

### What are the birthstones for autumn months?

September is sapphire, October is opal (and tourmaline), and November is topaz (and citrine). Sapphire, Opal, and Topaz are all used as given names.
